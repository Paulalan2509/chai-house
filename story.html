A corner café that never rushes you

Bazaar Chai House is built on one idea: a good glass of chai should take its time, and so should you.

It started with one stove and four marble tables on a street corner. People came for the chai and stayed for the buns that came out of the oven at seven every morning.

We still work the old way. The tea is simmered in the pot, never rushed. The bread is baked in the morning, never reheated. The prices stay low enough for students, taxi drivers and office workers to come every day.

Sit as long as you like. Nobody will bring you the bill until you ask for it.

How we make the chai

  1. Simmer

    Tea leaves boil in water until the decoction turns deep red.

  2. Add milk

    Full-cream milk and sugar go in, never straight from the fridge.

  3. Reduce slowly

    The pot stays on a low flame for an hour or more. It thickens and sweetens on its own.

  4. Pour from height

    Each glass is poured from a height. It cools the chai a little and gives it a light foam.

What we promise

Fresh every day

Stay as long as you like

No minimum order and no time limit. The corner table is yours for the afternoon.
