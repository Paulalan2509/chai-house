A day at the café

Every hour has
its own flavour.

Come early for the first tray, linger through the afternoon, or join the evening crowd.

06:30 — 11:00

First pour

The first trays come out around seven. The chai pot is on the stove from the minute we open.

11:00 — 17:00

Slow afternoons

Newspapers, laptops and long conversations. Filter coffee, light snacks and nobody hurrying you.

17:00 — 23:00

Evening adda

Samosas, lukhmi and a second round of chai. Tables fill up by seven, so reserve if you are a group.
